The rule, mirrored

Eight equal shares of the live pairs

A strength bucket is where a pair of hole cards ranks among every pair still possible on the board at that moment, cut into eight equal shares, 0 the weakest. Equal strengths share a bucket from the middle of their run. This is strategy::action::buckets (crates/strategy/src/action.rs:338-362), and the action model sees hole cards only through it.

five board cards and one hole pair, dealt from a fresh deck
—
On a river board there are 1081 live pairs. The exact three-way state would be 1081² = 1,168,561 opponent-pair combinations; the bucket state is 8² = 64. That is the whole reason a multiway re-solve fits a clock.

The tree and the clock

One street, eight buckets, a price in bucket visits

The tree mirrors resolve::tree (crates/strategy/src/resolve/tree.rs): a player not facing a bet may check, bet half the pot, bet the pot or go all in; facing one they may fold, call, raise the pot or go all in; at most maxBets bets and raises a street; a call or a check-around ends it. Chips start at the street, not the hand. The iteration count is iterations_for(pot, big_blind, cards) (crates/strategy/src/resolve.rs:40-47), 200–2000 on the river and a quarter of that before it.

The verdict

Go, three-way and bounded

A three-way river re-solve over the eight strength buckets clears the margin rule: at 200 paired 6-max bouts, 30% eligible, the pooled reading is +17.99 bb/100 with a ±10.37 95% radius, admitted under the 20 bb/100 rule. The 340-iteration solve is 59 ms at 100M bucket-visits/s (117 ms at 50M), about 1.5% of the 8 s cap. Four way fits; five and six way need one raise or one bet per street.

What would flip it

  • The eligible three-way river share below roughly 10%: the mean shrinks proportionally, and the margin becomes a loss bound again.
  • A row mix with more calling stations and maniacs: the free re-solve loses there; the pooled mean could fall below −20.
  • A tree cap that removes the raise branch: the challenger's edge over the pricer is largely the second opponent's response, not the re-raise.
  • A runtime more than ~8× slower than the 100M visits/s projection: the 8 s cap would bind on five way, not three.

The next rung

Implement the bounded three-way river re-solve behind the existing resolve seam (it returns None for opponents != 1 today), run the real Arena with 80–200 bouts, and pair it with E2's read-pinned form. Do not ship the free re-solve as the only multiway path: it checks the nuts against a station.

What the prototype does not establish

Limits, plainly

  • It is a bucket game, not the Arena. Showdown at bucket level with a continuous tie-break, independent opponent ranges, no card removal between opponents. The real re-solve works on 1081 pairs and a fitted action model.
  • It is a river only. No flop or turn, no run-outs, no continuation leaves. The real re-solve's flop and turn leaves cost four times an iteration (DEEP_COST = 4) and sample 16 run-outs.
  • It uses one population prior for the policy's range, and the row priors for the dealt buckets. The real bot reads a fitted model from its own store (ADR 0004).
  • It projects 30% of hands as three-way rivers. The real share is a measurement, not a constant; the margin mean scales with it.
  • It has no luck correction. The Arena's all-in correction removes some variance; this river model has none, so its intervals are conservative.
  • The Rust rate is a projection. 50M/100M visits/s are assumptions for the shared i7-4770K, not a benchmark. The measured JS rate is 13.9M visits/s.
  • No production code changed. This is a throwaway branch; the go is evidence for a build, not the build.
The free re-solve is the E3 shape, not E2. It is exploitable by the calling station and maniac because it solves an equilibrium, not a read. E2's pinned re-solve is the deployment shape the design already names.
Reproduce it

One page, one seed, one command

The full simulation is in this page. Open the console and run:

simulate({ bouts: 200, seed: 7, eligible: 0.3, arms: ["free"] })

The 200-bout run takes about 30 s in this page; it returns the same reading as the recorded card. The cost tab builds the tree from the same code. The artifact is committed on wayfinder/v11-102-multiway; it contains no keys, no store contents and no opponent data.
